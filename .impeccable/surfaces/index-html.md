## Scope

`index.html`, the whole one-page site. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience and action

Someone holding a broken plastic part that is not sold on its own, reading on a phone. They must believe this person can redraw their part from a photo and print it, and then send the photo on WhatsApp. Proof on hand: the real fuel-connector lock tab (STL and design notes) and the maker's portrait. Never claim city, shipping, prices, lead times, payment or hours.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the drawing sheet of the visitor's part. A broken part becomes a dimensioned drawing before it becomes a print. It refuses the 3D-printing landing default: dark hero with a glowing printer, service-card grid, numbered steps, accordion FAQ, floating green chat bubble.

OWN-WORLD: ABNT drawing sheet. Cool sulfite paper, india-ink linework with ISO line weights, graphite dimension lines, non-photo-blue construction lines, 45° section hatching as the heavy field, one red revision mark per screen and it is the action. ISO 3098 technical lettering (osifont, inclined, uppercase) for titles, dimensions and the title block; a plain engineering grotesk for reading. Sheet frame with the wider binding margin, first-angle views, NOTAS list, carimbo cells. Dark mode is the same sheet as a blueprint copy.

STORY: I understand they redraw and print parts that no longer exist to buy. I believe it because I see a real part drawn in millimeters, cut, and built in 132 layers. I send a photo and a measurement on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: the brand line, then the headline "Não acha a peça? Eu desenho e imprimo." in inclined technical lettering at display size, one sentence, the red action "Mandar foto da peça". Right seven columns: the lock tab drawn from its STL in first-angle front, top and side views with its real overall dimensions, plus an isometric view that prints itself layer by layer. The carimbo sits fixed at the bottom right holding brand, drawn-by, scale, unit, sheet and the red action; on phones it is a bottom bar.

FORM: Folha de desenho técnico (position 4 of 7 on the grounded list; seed d0c5ded5). Raises kept: registered layers over a fixed keyline (woodblock); the order prints itself into the carimbo (terminal); one continuous sheet, no stacked cards (ebru); the hatched cut is the color field (daylight section); red marks only the action (rain city). Signature interaction: the part drawing builds in registered layers (keyline, dimensions, hatch, print layers), then turns under the pointer.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
